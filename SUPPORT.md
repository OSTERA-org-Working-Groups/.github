# Getting Help

## Comments on Draft Documents

To comment on a draft, open an issue in that repository using the Work Item Comment template in that repository. During a Public Review, comments can also be sent to [public-review@ostera.org](mailto:public-review@ostera.org).

## Questions

For general questions about OSTERA, visit [OSTERA.org](https://ostera.org/) or contact the Secretariat at [secretariat@ostera.org](mailto:secretariat@ostera.org).

## Security Issues

Follow the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) and do not open a public issue.
